Everything in one workspace

Professional timeline editing, AI generation, transcript-driven cuts, and agent automation — without leaving your machine.

🎬

Multitrack timeline

Unlimited video and audio tracks with transitions, effects, LUTs, zoom envelopes, keyframes, and markers. Full undo/redo history.

📝

Transcript-driven editing

Word-level transcription with text-based cuts. Strike through words to delete them. Pause compression, speaker labels, and unified search.

💬

AI captions

Automatic captions from transcript data. TikTok, YouTube, and custom styles with karaoke highlighting. SRT, VTT, and embedded export.

🎨

AI generation

Generate images, video, speech, music, and sound effects from text. Powered by Google Vertex AI (Imagen, Veo), Gemini, Pollinations, ElevenLabs, and more. Background job queue with live progress and retry.

🤖

Agent-native

Built-in conversational agent with proposal-based edits. Connect external agents via MCP — Codex, Claude Code, and more.

🎵

Audio intelligence

Auto-ducking, vocal isolation, loudness normalization, beat detection, and music-synced cuts. Multiple audio tracks with full mixing.

📐

Visual intelligence

In-browser person segmentation, face-safe caption zones, auto-reframe for aspect ratio conversion, and AI-assisted color grading.

📦

Production-ready export

MP4 with hardware-accelerated H.264, separate audio, captions, FCPXML for DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro, and full project archives.

🔒

Local-first

Projects and media stay on your machine by default. API keys remain server-side. Core editing works fully offline.

Powered by the best AI providers

Google Vertex AI

Imagen image generation, Veo 3.1 Lite video generation, and Gemini TTS — all using GCP credits. Premium quality with ADC authentication.

✨

Free AI chat (Gemini 3.7 Flash), transcription, and TTS (gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview). Just add your API key.

ElevenLabs & more

Premium voice synthesis via ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Cartesia, MiniMax, Doubao, and others. Bring your own API key.
