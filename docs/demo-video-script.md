# Lazynext Demo Video Script

A 90-second demo video showing the core AI video editing workflow.
Designed to be recorded with screen capture software (OBS, QuickTime, or Loom).

---

## Setup Before Recording

1. Launch Lazynext desktop app (v0.4.2 or later)
2. Have a sample project loaded with:
   - A short video clip (30-60 seconds) with dialogue
   - Transcript already generated
3. Set screen resolution to 1920x1080
4. Close all other applications
5. Use a clean desktop wallpaper

---

## Script

### Scene 1: Hook (0:00 - 0:10)

**Visual**: Full-screen view of the Lazynext editor with a loaded project.
Timeline visible with clips. AI chat panel open on the right.

**Voiceover**:
"What if you could edit video just by talking to AI? Not a chatbot that describes edits — an AI agent that actually makes them on your timeline."

**On-screen text**: "Lazynext — AI-Native Video Editor"

---

### Scene 2: AI Chat Edit (0:10 - 0:35)

**Visual**: Click on the AI chat panel. Type a command.

**Action**: Type: "Add a title card at the beginning that says 'Product Demo'"

**Voiceover**:
"Ask the AI agent to make an edit. It proposes a plan, you approve it, and the edit lands on your timeline as a real, editable clip."

**Visual**: Show the agent's proposal appearing, then click "Approve".
Show the title clip appearing on the timeline.

---

### Scene 3: Transcript Editing (0:35 - 0:55)

**Visual**: Switch to the Transcript view.

**Action**: Strike through a few words in the transcript to delete them from the video.

**Voiceover**:
"Edit video by editing text. Strike through words in the transcript to cut them from the timeline. Pause compression, speaker labels, and unified search make finding the right moment effortless."

**Visual**: Show the words being struck through, then the timeline updating to reflect the cuts.

---

### Scene 4: AI Generation (0:55 - 1:15)

**Visual**: Switch back to AI chat.

**Action**: Type: "Generate background music that matches the mood of this video"

**Voiceover**:
"Lazynext integrates with Google Vertex AI, Gemini, Pollinations, ElevenLabs, and more. Generate images, video, speech, music, and sound effects — all from chat."

**Visual**: Show the generation progress, then the music track appearing on the timeline. Play a few seconds to hear it.

---

### Scene 5: Captions (1:15 - 1:30)

**Visual**: Switch to the Captions view.

**Action**: Apply a TikTok-style caption template.

**Voiceover**:
"Automatic captions from your transcript. TikTok, YouTube, and custom styles with karaoke highlighting. Export to SRT, VTT, or embedded in your video."

**Visual**: Show captions appearing on the video preview with the karaoke highlight effect.

---

### Scene 6: Export & Call to Action (1:30 - 1:45)

**Visual**: Click Export, choose MP4, show the export progress.

**Voiceover**:
"Export to MP4 with hardware acceleration, FCPXML for DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro, or full project archives. Local-first — everything stays on your machine."

**Visual**: Export completes. Show the final video playing.

**On-screen text**:
"Free and open source"
"Download at lazynext.com"
"GitHub: github.com/Lazynext-Platform/Lazynext"

**Voiceover**:
"Lazynext is free, open source, and available for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Download today at lazynext.com."

## Recording Tips

- **Keep it under 90 seconds** — attention spans are short
- **Show, don't tell** — every voiceover line should match an on-screen action
- **Use a real project** — don't use empty timelines or placeholder content
- **Record at 1080p 60fps** for smooth playback
- **Add background music** at low volume (-20dB) during editing scenes
- **Use the Lazynext logo** as a watermark in the bottom-right corner
- **Add subtitles** to the demo video itself (practice what you preach!)

## Editing the Demo

1. Trim any pauses or mistakes
2. Add the Lazynext logo intro (1 second)
3. Add the on-screen text overlays
4. Add background music
5. Add captions
6. Export as 1080p MP4 (H.264, 10Mbps)
7. Upload to YouTube and pin to the @lazynext channel
